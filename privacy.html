Cockroach Run: Delhi Escape: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Cockroach Run: Delhi Escape ("the game") collects and how it is used.

Information the game keeps on your device

Your scores, coins, stars, purchases made with in-game coins and settings are saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this as described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.

The game also shows sponsor artwork inside the game world (for example on billboards). These images are downloaded from GitHub and do not collect any information about you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes are posted on this page with a new date.
